Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to investigate a report?

Investigation timelines vary depending on the complexity and nature of the fraud. We typically acknowledge receipt of your report within 24 hours.

Will my identity be kept confidential?

Yes, we take confidentiality seriously. You can choose to remain anonymous, and we will protect your personal information in accordance with our privacy policy.

What information should I provide?

The more details you provide, the better we can assist. Include dates, amounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers, websites, transaction details, and any communications with the suspect.

Can I report anonymously?

Yes. Select "Anonymous" in the Reporter Type field. Note that without contact information, we may have limited ability to follow up with you.

What happens after I submit my report?

Your report will be reviewed by our team. Depending on the nature of the fraud, it may be forwarded to relevant law enforcement or regulatory agencies.

Is this website secure?

Yes, all communications are encrypted and your data is handled securely. We do not sell or share your information with third parties.
