Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a refund typically take?

All refunds are processed to the original credit card used for the transaction. Credit card refunds typically appear within 3-5 business days. Our review process typically takes 5-7 business days before processing.

Can I track my refund status?

Yes! Use the "Track Refund" section above to check the status of your request. You'll need your Request ID and the email address associated with your request.

What if my refund is denied?

If your refund request is denied, we will contact you with a detailed explanation. You can appeal the decision by providing additional documentation or information.

What information do I need to provide?

You'll need the transaction date, amount, merchant name, transaction ID, and a detailed reason for your refund request. Supporting documents like receipts or invoices are helpful.

Can I request a refund for a credit card dispute?

If you've already filed a dispute with your credit card company, we recommend following their process first. However, you can also submit a refund request here for our investigation.

Is my financial information secure?

Yes. All communications are encrypted and we never store complete financial information. Your data is handled according to our privacy policy and PCI DSS standards.
